Questions we hear a lot

Straight answers

Do you write commercial trucking insurance?

Yes — it is our specialty. We work with owner-operators and small fleets on primary liability, physical damage, motor truck cargo, non-trucking liability and general liability, and because we are a broker we quote it across carriers that specialize in trucking rather than forcing one company’s appetite onto your operation.

Commercial trucking coverage →

How much does insurance cost in Claremore?

Nobody can answer that honestly without your details, and you should be skeptical of any site that tries. Auto premiums turn on drivers, vehicles, history, limits and deductibles. Home premiums turn on rebuild cost, roof age, construction and claims history. What we can promise is a real quote, usually same day, with the assumptions written down so you can compare it fairly against what you have now.

Do I have to come into the office?

No. We have a full office in Claremore and you are welcome there, but most of our clients never need to visit — quotes, applications and signatures all happen by phone, email and e-signature, the same way a standard insurance agency works. If you would rather sit down in person, or need us to come out to your location, we can arrange that too.

What makes a "financials" agency different from a regular one?

A property and casualty agency stops at protecting things you own. We also work on the money side: life insurance sized to real obligations, final expense, annuities and retirement income, and business planning. Those decisions interact — how much life insurance you need depends on what you have saved, and what you can afford to save depends on what your premiums cost. Handling both at once produces better answers than handling each in isolation.

Can you review my policies without me switching anything?

Yes, and we do it constantly. Send or hand over your declarations pages and we will read them with you. Sometimes the honest answer is that what you have is solid and priced well, and we will say so. A review that ends in "keep what you have" is still worth the hour.
