Before you call

What helps us quote faster

None of this is required — we can work from very little. But if you have it on hand, these details turn a two-call process into one.

For auto

  • Year, make and model of each vehicle
  • VINs if you have them
  • Names and license numbers of all drivers
  • Accidents or tickets in the last five years

For home

  • Square footage and year built
  • Roof age and material
  • Heat source and construction type
  • Detached buildings with rough sizes

For life insurance

  • Date of birth and tobacco use
  • Mortgage balance and other debts
  • Household income
  • Any coverage you already hold

For retirement

  • Statements for existing accounts
  • Approximate retirement date
  • Expected Social Security or pension income
  • What you want the money to do

Already insured elsewhere?

Send the declarations page — the summary listing your coverages and limits. That one document lets us match your coverage exactly instead of guessing, which makes the comparison honest on both sides.

You are not obligated to switch anything. Plenty of these reviews end with us saying the current policy is priced well and structured correctly. That is still a useful answer.

Timing tip: reach out two to four weeks before your renewal date. It leaves room to compare properly rather than deciding under pressure.
